# PRIVACY POLICY

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to WheelWin.

Your privacy is important to us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected while you use WheelWin.

By using WheelWin, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

WheelWin may collect the following information:

* Nickname
* Language preference
* Telegram Wallet address
* Game session information
* Connection status
* Technical diagnostics and error logs

WheelWin does **not** collect passwords or private wallet keys.

---

## 3. Wallet Information

WheelWin supports only Telegram Wallet.

The application uses your wallet address solely for:

* verifying participation,
* receiving entry payments,
* sending prize payouts,
* maintaining game records.

Private keys and wallet recovery phrases are never requested or stored.

---

## 4. Blockchain Data

Transactions on the TON blockchain are public by nature.

WheelWin may store references to blockchain transactions, including:

* transaction hashes,
* payment status,
* settlement status,
* contract identifiers.

Blockchain data cannot be modified after confirmation.

---

## 5. Game Data

During gameplay, WheelWin stores information necessary to operate the game, including:

* room identifier,
* player identifiers,
* selected sectors,
* game configuration,
* payment status,
* winner information,
* audit records.

This information is used only for operating and verifying games.

---

## 6. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:

* operate multiplayer games,
* verify payments,
* determine winners,
* distribute prizes,
* recover interrupted sessions,
* improve system reliability,
* investigate technical issues.

---

## 7. Data Sharing

WheelWin does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only when necessary to:

* process blockchain transactions,
* operate Telegram Wallet integration,
* comply with applicable legal obligations.

---

## 8. Security

WheelWin is designed with security as a core principle.

Security measures include:

* Server Authoritative architecture
* Immutable game configuration
* Blockchain payment verification
* Secure audit logging
* Session validation

Although reasonable security measures are used, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

---

## 9. Cookies and Local Storage

WheelWin may use local browser storage to remember:

* language preference,
* interface settings,
* temporary session information.

No sensitive wallet credentials are stored in browser storage.

---

## 10. Third-Party Services

WheelWin may interact with third-party services including:

* Telegram Wallet
* TON blockchain infrastructure
* Network providers required for blockchain communication

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

---

## 11. Data Retention

Game-related information may be retained for:

* audit purposes,
* payment verification,
* dispute investigation,
* system integrity.

Retention periods may vary depending on operational and legal requirements.

---

## 12. Children's Privacy

WheelWin is intended only for users who are legally permitted to participate under applicable laws.

The service is not designed for children.

---

## 13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

The latest version will always be available within the application.

Continued use of WheelWin constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

---

## 14. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the official WheelWin communication channels available on the Welcome page.
