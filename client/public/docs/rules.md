# WHEELWIN RULES

## Welcome to WheelWin!

WheelWin is a **peer-versus-peer** game where **three real players** compete against each other.

The server never plays and never changes the outcome. It acts only as an impartial referee.

---

## How to Play

1. Create or join a game room.
2. Enter your nickname.
3. Choose your sectors on the wheel.
4. Connect your Telegram Wallet.
5. Confirm your entry payment.
6. Wait until all three players complete payment.
7. The game starts automatically.

---

## Players

* Exactly **3 players** participate in every game.
* Every player chooses **1 or 2 sectors**.
* Every sector has **exactly the same probability** of winning.
* There are **no bonus sectors**, **no jackpot sectors**, and **no hidden multipliers**.

---

## Entry Payment

All players choose the same base stake:

* **1 GRM**, or
* **10 GRM**

If a player buys a second sector, its price is **1.5×** the base stake.

Payments are made only through **Telegram Wallet**.

---

## Prize Distribution

After all payments are confirmed:

* **95%** of the total prize pool is paid to the winner.
* **5%** is the organizer fee.

Prize distribution is performed automatically by the smart contract.

---

## Fair Play

WheelWin is built on the following principles:

* Server Authoritative
* Deterministic Game Logic
* Equal Winning Chances
* Immutable Game Configuration
* Blockchain Payment Verification

No player can change the wheel after the game starts.

---

## Disconnections

If a player disconnects:

* the game continues under server control;
* reconnect is supported whenever possible;
* if an offline player wins, the prize is still transferred to their wallet.

---

## Security

* Only Telegram Wallet is supported.
* Payments are verified on the TON blockchain.
* All financial operations are recorded in an immutable audit log.

---

## Good Luck!

Every purchased sector has the same chance to win.

Choose wisely and enjoy the game! 🎯
