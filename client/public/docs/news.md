Fair Gameplay System

WheelWin follows several core principles to ensure every game is fair:

Equal probability for every purchased sector
Immutable game configuration
Deterministic game simulation
Server-authoritative gameplay
Blockchain payment verification

Fairness remains our highest priority.

Smart Contract Settlement

Prize distribution is handled automatically after the game ends.

Winner receives 95% of the prize pool.
Organizer receives 5%.

All settlements are verified through the TON blockchain.

Telegram Wallet Support

WheelWin supports secure wallet connection through Telegram Wallet.

Players can:

connect their wallet;
confirm entry payments;
receive prize payouts automatically.

Private keys are never requested or stored.
